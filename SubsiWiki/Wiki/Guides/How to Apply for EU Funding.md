# How to Apply for EU Funding

This guide summarizes the generic process for funds directly managed by the [[European Commission]]. Always follow the specific call guidelines.

## Checklist

1. **Find a relevant call** — for Commission-managed calls and tenders, start with the [[Funding & Tenders Portal]].
2. **Read the call guidelines carefully** — check scope, criteria, documents, templates, deadline, submission route, and page limits.
3. **Confirm eligibility** — eligibility is programme- and call-specific.
4. **Check whether the call is one-stage or two-stage**:
   - One-stage: submit the full application immediately.
   - Two-stage: submit a summary proposal first; if shortlisted, submit a full proposal later.
5. **Decide whether to apply alone or with partners** — most opportunities are collaborative, but ERC and MSCA routes may support individuals, teams, or organisations.
6. **Prepare the proposal early** — proposal preparation is time-consuming.
7. **Use the required documents/templates** — failure to follow guidelines can make the proposal inadmissible.
8. **Submit electronically and on time**.
9. **Check admissibility** — submissions must be on time, complete, and concise within the stated page limit.
