## Programmes and funds

- [[Horizon Europe]] — EU research and innovation programme for 2021–2027, budget €95.5 billion.
- [[EU4Health]] — health programme implemented by HaDEA, budget €5.3 billion.
- [[Cohesion Fund]] — support for countries below 90% of EU average GNI per inhabitant.
- [[LIFE Programme]] — environment, climate, circular economy, nature, biodiversity, and clean energy transition.
- [[European Regional Development Fund]] — regional cohesion with emphasis including innovation and research.
- [[European Structural and Investment Funds]] — five jointly managed funds for jobs, sustainability, economy, and environment.
- [[Research Fund for Coal and Steel]] — coal and steel research funding, around €55 million annually.
- [[Connecting Europe Facility]] — funding for energy, transport, and ICT networks.
- [[Common Agricultural Policy]] — agriculture-related funding including direct payments and rural development.

## Entities and portals

- [[European Commission]] — central EU institution for direct management and oversight of shared management.
- [[Funding & Tenders Portal]] — single entry point for EU funding and procurement opportunities managed by the European Commission.
- [[EISMEA]] — agency managing Single Market Programme SME-related actions.
- [[CINEA]] — agency managing CEF strands and LIFE climate-related activity.
- [[HaDEA]] — agency implementing EU4Health and some digital/health-related programmes.
