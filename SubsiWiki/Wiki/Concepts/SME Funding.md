# SME Funding

Small and medium-sized enterprises can use several EU funding routes depending on sector, location, and project type.

## Main routes

- **Access to finance and markets**: the Single Market Programme, managed by [[EISMEA]], aims to improve SMEs' access to finance and markets.
- **Infrastructure networks**: [[Connecting Europe Facility]] can fund SME-relevant energy, transport, and ICT projects.
- **Research and innovation**: [[Horizon Europe]] supports research projects by organisations and individuals.
- **Regional development**: cohesion policy supports SMEs through national/regional management of cohesion funding.
- **Agriculture**: [[Common Agricultural Policy]] includes schemes and funds that small companies can access.
- **Training and competitiveness**: ESF+ can support SME competitiveness, including training.
- **Climate and green commercialization**: [[LIFE Programme]] can support close-to-market green products, technologies, services, and processes.
- **Microfinance**: EaSI Microfinance Facility provides loans up to €25,000 for individuals setting up or developing small businesses.

## Practical implication

SMEs should first identify whether their need is a project grant, public tender, financial instrument, regional/national subsidy, research collaboration, or sector-specific route.
