# Research and Innovation Funding

Research and innovation funding includes programmes/open calls, fellowships, individual research grants, prizes, youth/science contests, seals of excellence, talent events, tenders, and project-management support.

## Main programme family

- [[Horizon Europe]] is the flagship EU research and innovation programme for 2021–2027.
- Previous frameworks include Horizon 2020 and FP7.

## Other programme/fund routes

- [[EU4Health]]
- [[Cohesion Fund]]
- [[LIFE Programme]]
- [[European Regional Development Fund]]
- [[European Structural and Investment Funds]]
- [[Research Fund for Coal and Steel]]

## Individual and prize routes

- ERC grants for frontier research.
- Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions fellowships.
- EU research and innovation prizes such as EIC Horizon Prizes and other thematic prizes.

## Workflow

Use [[Funding & Tenders Portal]] for open/upcoming calls when available, confirm eligibility and admissibility, and follow [[How to Apply for EU Funding]].
