# EU Funding Management Modes

## Direct management

The [[European Commission]] directly manages the programme lifecycle: calls, evaluation, grant agreements, monitoring, results assessment, and payments. Applicants usually apply by responding to calls for proposals, often via the [[Funding & Tenders Portal]].

## Shared management

The Commission and national authorities jointly manage funding. Around 70% of EU programmes use this mode. National, regional, and local administrations choose projects and run day-to-day management, while the Commission works with countries to ensure delivery and proper spending.

Common shared-management areas include cohesion policy and agriculture, including the [[European Regional Development Fund]], [[Cohesion Fund]], ESF+, Just Transition Fund, European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development, and fisheries-related funds.

## Indirect management

Third parties such as national authorities or international organisations implement the funding. The source notes this is common for humanitarian aid and international development.
