# EU Funding Types

## Types

- **Grants** — usually awarded after applicants submit project ideas under a call for proposals.
- **Subsidies** — often managed by national or regional authorities.
- **Loans, guarantees, and equity** — financial assistance supporting EU policies and programmes.
- **Loans to states** — loans to EU Member States and non-EU countries.
- **Prizes** — awarded to winners of EU contests, including Horizon Europe-related contests.
- **Tenders / public contracts** — procurement where EU bodies buy goods or services through calls for tender.

## Research and innovation examples

Research and innovation opportunities include [[Horizon Europe]], ERC grants, MSCA fellowships, prizes, EUCYS, Seal of Excellence, and EU TalentOn.
