# EU Funding Overview

EU funding covers grants, subsidies, financial instruments, prizes, tenders, and programme-specific support. For many directly managed programmes, the practical starting point is the [[Funding & Tenders Portal]], while shared-management funds often require national or regional portals and authorities.

## Main routes

- [[EU Funding Types|Grants and calls for proposals]]: common for projects furthering EU priorities.
- [[EU Funding Types|Tenders and public contracts]]: procurement where EU institutions buy goods or services.
- [[EU Funding Types|Financial instruments]]: loans, guarantees, equity, microfinance, and related access-to-finance tools.
- [[EU Funding Types|Prizes]]: contest-like funding, especially in research and innovation.
- [[EU Funding Management Modes|Shared-management funding]]: national/regional authorities select projects, especially for cohesion and agriculture.

## Where to look first

1. For Commission-managed calls and tenders, use [[Funding & Tenders Portal]].
2. For research and innovation, start with [[Research and Innovation Funding]] and [[Horizon Europe]].
3. For small businesses, use [[SME Funding]].
4. For regional/cohesion routes, check [[EU Funding Management Modes]] and national/regional authorities.
5. For direct applications, follow [[How to Apply for EU Funding]].
