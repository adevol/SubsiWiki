# Funding & Tenders Portal

The Funding & Tenders Portal is the single entry point / Single Electronic Data Interchange Area for applicants, contractors, and experts in European Commission-managed funding programmes and procurements.

Use it to discover opportunities, browse EU programmes, access reference documents and eligibility criteria, follow participant guidance, and find open calls/tenders.
