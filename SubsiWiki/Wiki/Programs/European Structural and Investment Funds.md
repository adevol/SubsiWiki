# European Structural and Investment Funds

Over half of EU funding is channelled through five European Structural and Investment Funds, jointly managed by the [[European Commission]] and EU countries. The funds invest in job creation and a sustainable, healthy economy and environment.

The listed funds include ERDF, ESF, Cohesion Fund, EAFRD, and EMFF/EMFAF-related fisheries funding.
