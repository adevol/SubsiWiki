# Cohesion Fund

The Cohesion Fund targets EU countries whose gross national income per inhabitant is below 90% of the EU average. It aims to reduce economic and social disparities and promote sustainable development.

It is also one of the funds associated with [[EU Funding Management Modes|shared management]].
