# Horizon Europe

Horizon Europe is the EU's research and innovation programme for 2021–2027 with a budget of €95.5 billion.

## Purpose

It tackles climate change, supports the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and boosts EU competitiveness and growth. It facilitates collaboration and strengthens the impact of research and innovation in EU policy and global-challenge work.

## Relevant to

- Research organisations and individuals.
- SMEs with research and innovation projects.
- Applicants looking for open and upcoming calls via the [[Funding & Tenders Portal]].
