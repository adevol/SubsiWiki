# Source - Funding programmes and open calls

## Summary

This page lists important EU funding programmes and open-call families relevant to research and innovation.

## Extracted programme facts

- [[Horizon Europe]]: EU research and innovation programme for 2021–2027; budget €95.5 billion; supports climate action, UN Sustainable Development Goals, competitiveness, growth, collaboration, policy impact, knowledge/technology diffusion, jobs, talent, industrial competitiveness, and the European Research Area.
- [[EU4Health]]: implemented by [[HaDEA]]; budget €5.3 billion; fourth and largest EU health programme since launch in 2003.
- [[Cohesion Fund]]: for EU countries whose GNI per inhabitant is below 90% of the EU average; aims to reduce economic/social disparities and promote sustainable development.
- [[LIFE Programme]]: 2021–2027 sub-programmes include Nature and biodiversity, Circular economy and quality of life, Climate change mitigation and adaptation, and Clean energy transition.
- [[European Regional Development Fund]]: strengthens economic and social cohesion by correcting regional imbalances; priority areas include innovation and research.
- [[European Structural and Investment Funds]]: over half of EU funding goes through five funds jointly managed by the Commission and EU countries.
- [[Research Fund for Coal and Steel]]: supports coal and steel sector research; around €55 million annually.
