# Source - Funding grants subsidies European Union

## Summary

EU funding supports projects and programmes under strict rules intended to ensure transparent and accountable use of funds. The page explains the main forms of EU funding and the three management modes.

## Key points

- Main funding forms: [[EU Funding Types|grants]], subsidies, loans/guarantees/equity, loans to states, prizes, and public procurement-related opportunities.
- [[EU Funding Management Modes|Direct management]] means the [[European Commission]] runs the call lifecycle: launches calls, evaluates proposals, signs grant agreements, monitors implementation, assesses results, and makes payments.
- Shared management means the Commission and national authorities jointly manage funding; around 70% of EU programmes are run this way.
- Shared management is prominent in cohesion policy and agriculture.
- Indirect management means third parties such as national authorities or international organisations implement programmes; it is important in humanitarian aid and international development.
