# Source - The application process

## Summary

A generic description of steps for applying to funds directly managed by the European Commission.

## Key points

- Funding procedures may have one or two stages.
- One-stage procedure: submit the full application at the outset.
- Two-stage procedure: submit an initial summary proposal; shortlisted applicants are invited to submit a full application in phase two.
- Applicants should identify a relevant call, read guidelines carefully, and check criteria/requirements before proceeding.
- Eligibility is call-specific and must be confirmed in call guidelines.
- Most funding opportunities are collaborative projects, but individual researchers, teams, or organisations can apply via ERC and MSCA routes.
- Proposal preparation is time-consuming; all required documents and templates are listed in the call guidelines.
- Failure to follow call guidelines can make a proposal inadmissible.
- Admissibility requires on-time electronic submission, completeness, and concision within the proposal-template page limit.
