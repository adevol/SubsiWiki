# Source - Funding

## Summary

A research-and-innovation funding navigation page that points users to opportunities, application guidance, partner search, project-selection information, project management guidance, and tender opportunities.

## Key points

- Funding opportunities include programmes/open calls, fellowships, prizes, EUCYS, Seal of Excellence, and EU TalentOn.
- Application guidance includes the application process and how to apply for funding.
- Partner support includes finding project partners and Horizon Europe partner/individual application guidance.
- Selection guidance points to Horizon Europe admissibility and eligibility checks.
- Project management guidance includes Horizon Europe recordkeeping and European Commission project-management pages.
- Research and innovation tenders include open/closed calls, contract award notices, activity reports, and work programmes.
