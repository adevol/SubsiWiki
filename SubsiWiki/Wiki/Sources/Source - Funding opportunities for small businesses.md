# Source - Funding opportunities for small businesses

## Summary

The page maps EU funding opportunities for small businesses across access to finance/markets, infrastructure networks, research and innovation, regional funding, agriculture, social funding, fisheries/aquaculture, climate, and development.

## Key points

- The Single Market Programme aims to improve SMEs' access to finance and markets and is managed by [[EISMEA]].
- SMEs are eligible under the [[Connecting Europe Facility]], which funds energy, transport, and ICT projects; strands are managed by [[CINEA]] and [[HaDEA]].
- [[Horizon Europe]] supports research projects by organisations or individuals.
- Cohesion policy invests in regions, cities, and the real economy to stimulate growth and jobs; supporting SMEs is a key priority, and each EU country manages cohesion funding on its territory.
- [[Common Agricultural Policy]] routes for small companies include school fruit/vegetables/milk, promotion of EU farm products, direct payments via the European Agricultural Guarantee Fund, and the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development.
- Additional SME routes include grants/calls for proposals, public contracts/calls for tender, Access to finance portal, EaSI Microfinance Facility loans up to €25,000, ESF+ training/competitiveness support, EMFAF, LIFE close-to-market projects, and development funding.
