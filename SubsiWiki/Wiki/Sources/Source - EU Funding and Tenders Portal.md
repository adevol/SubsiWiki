# Source - EU Funding and Tenders Portal

## Summary

The Funding & Tenders Portal is described as the single entry point / Single Electronic Data Interchange Area for applicants, contractors, and experts dealing with funding programmes and procurements managed by the European Commission.

## Key points

- Main discovery point for EU funding and tender opportunities.
- Provides guidance on how to participate.
- Lists EU programmes under the 2021–2027 multiannual financial framework by heading and cluster.
- Gives access to programme information such as reference documents, eligibility criteria, participant guidance, and project results.
- Contains support news and events.
- The source notes a mandatory two-factor authentication rollout for EU Login accounts.
