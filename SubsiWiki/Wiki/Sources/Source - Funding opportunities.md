# Source - Funding opportunities

## Summary

A directory of research and innovation funding opportunity categories.

## Key points

- Main category: funding programmes and open calls.
- Individual/fellowship routes include ERC grants and Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions.
- Prize routes include EU Award for Gender Equality Champions, EIC Horizon Prizes, SOFT Innovation Prize, energy-community governance prize, and Nuclear Innovation Prize.
- Other opportunity categories include EU Contest for Young Scientists, Seal of Excellence, and EU TalentOn.
